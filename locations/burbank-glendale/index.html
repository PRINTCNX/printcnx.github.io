Your Local Commercial Printer

Burbank and Glendale represent the historic center of Southern California's film, television, and broadcast production. From major studio lots like Warner Bros. and The Walt Disney Studios to hundreds of animation houses, post-production suites, and PR agencies lining Olive Avenue and Brand Boulevard, production schedules here do not allow for late deliveries.

The Printing Connection has produced entertainment print since 1983. We understand that screening dates, cast lookbooks, red-carpet step-and-repeats, and press kits cannot miss their curtain time. With daily deliveries running across the 101/134 corridor directly into Burbank and Glendale, media marketers receive responsive pressroom access without central-city overhead.

Burbank & Glendale Printing FAQs

Can you handle urgent premiere or screening print deadlines in Burbank?+

Yes. Entertainment deadlines are a cornerstone of our pressroom. We operate high-speed digital presses daily and coordinate same-day emergency courier delivery directly to studio gates and screening rooms.

Do you sign non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) for unreleased entertainment titles?+

Standard operating procedure. We routinely sign NDAs and handle sensitive pre-release marketing materials, lookbooks, and festival screeners under strict confidential production protocols.

Can you match specific Pantone spot colors on entertainment press kits?+

Yes. We perform weekly spectrophotometer calibration on our digital presses in Newbury Park and manage commercial sheetfed offset runs with per-job color consistency checks and true Pantone PMS spot-color accuracy.
