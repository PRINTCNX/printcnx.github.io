Pre-Press

Digital Workflow & Pre-Press Technology

Our completely digital pre-press workflow reduces waste, improves accuracy, and enhances print quality through rigorous, built-in quality checks at every step.

Mac Pro Workstations

Dedicated design and prepress stations for Macintosh-native workflows (Adobe CC, InDesign, Illustrator, Photoshop).

PC Workstations

Windows-based prepress stations supporting all major Windows-native file formats and applications.

Dedicated Web & File Servers

Secure, high-speed file servers for FTP, web portal upload, and physical media — supporting any file size.

Epson 9900 Color Proofer (44" wide)

Wide-format Epson inkjet proofing system used for full-size, color-accurate proofs before press runs. ISO 12647-7 compliant.

Fuji Proofing System

Fuji certified contract proofing — the industry standard for offset press approval. Simulates final press output on the actual substrate.

Creo RIP

Industry-leading Creo raster image processor delivers precise color separation, accurate screening, and reliable plate output.

Computer to Plate (CTP) System

Fully digital plate-making — no film. CTP eliminates intermediate steps, increases accuracy, and reduces press make-ready time and cost.

Bindery & Finishing

Bindery & Finishing Equipment

From saddle-stitching to die-cutting to shrink wrapping — finishing capabilities handled with precision for faster turnaround and consistent quality.

Heidelberg Stitcher/Trimmer

Saddle stitch binding and three-knife trimming in one automated pass. Produces perfect booklets from 8 to 64 pages with square, accurate spines.

MBO Folders (×2)

Two MBO buckle/combination folding systems. Handles all standard fold styles: tri-fold, Z-fold, gate fold, roll fold, and custom configurations.

Heidelberg Die Cutter SBB (22" × 32")

Industrial Heidelberg die cutter for precision die-cutting, embossing, and debossing on sheets up to 22×32". Used for pocket folders, custom shapes, packaging, and specialty pieces.

Heidelberg Windmill

Classic Heidelberg Windmill platen press for precision die-cutting, scoring, and perforating on lighter stock. Ideal for business cards, hang tags, and scored folding pieces.

Kluge Die Cutter / Foil Stamper / Embosser

14" × 22" Kluge platen press for precision die-cutting, foil stamping, and embossing. Adds metallic foil, raised emboss, and custom die-cut shapes to business cards, covers, and luxury packaging — no digital file required, traditional craftsmanship with production throughput.

Programmable Cutters (×2)

Two programmable guillotine cutters for precise, repeatable trimming. CNC-programmed for complex cut sequences with accuracy to within 0.1mm.

Multiple Drill Presses (×2)

Precision drilling for ring-bound manuals, binders, and note pads. Single and ganged hole patterns drilled clean and accurate.

Shrink Wrapper

Polyethylene shrink wrapping for bundled delivery, retail packaging, and dust-protected print storage. Handles single items, stacked bundles, and palletized runs.

Computer to Plate (CTP) & GraCol Color Management

Our fully digital Computer to Plate workflow eliminates film entirely — plates are imaged directly from digital files, reducing make-ready time and press waste. Combined with our GraCol-based color management, weekly press calibration, and Fuji contract proofing, every job arrives on press pre-tuned to deliver accurate, predictable color.

The result: Your brand colors — Pantone PMS or CMYK — reproduced accurately and consistently across every run, every reorder.
