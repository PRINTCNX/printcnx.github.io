Our Mission Statement

What we believe. How we operate. Why we exist.

"
Our mission is to be the most trusted commercial printing partner for businesses in Southern California, Seattle, and beyond — delivering premium print quality, unmatched service, and innovative solutions that help our clients communicate their brand with confidence.

What We Believe

At The Printing Connection, we believe that exceptional printing is more than ink on paper — it is the physical embodiment of your brand's promise to your customers. When a guest at a luxury hotel picks up a welcome booklet, when a potential client opens your corporate brochure, when a trade show attendee holds a lenticular display that stops them in their tracks — that moment of tactile engagement is a moment of trust.

We take that trust seriously. Every project that leaves our facility reflects not only our technical capabilities but our commitment to your success.

How We Operate

We are a relationship-first business. We don't simply take orders — we consult, we advise, and we partner. When you bring a project to The Printing Connection, you get a dedicated team that understands your brand standards, your production schedule, and the competitive environment you operate in.

Our state-of-the-art production capabilities include digital printing, offset printing, wide format, lenticular printing, specialty finishing, and custom promotional products. We have invested in both the technology and the talent to handle complex, high-stakes commercial printing projects with consistency and care.

Our Commitment to Our Clients

  • Quality you can see and feel — We use premium paper stocks, precise color management, and meticulous finishing on every job.
  • Honest, transparent communication — If there's a challenge with your file or your timeline, we'll tell you immediately — not after the deadline has passed.
  • Responsive service — Our clients reach real people who know their accounts. Not a ticketing system. Not a chatbot.
  • Continuous improvement — We constantly invest in our equipment, our processes, and our people to stay ahead of the industry.
  • Community — Based in Newbury Park, CA, we are proud members of the Conejo Valley business community and active supporters of local organizations and charitable causes.

Our Promise

Whether you are a local Ventura County small business or a national hotel group with properties across the country, you will receive the same dedication, the same quality standards, and the same commitment to getting it right.

That is our mission. That is our promise. That is The Printing Connection.
