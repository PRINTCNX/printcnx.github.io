Setting the Record Straight

Dispelling the Myths About Print

Print has an unfair environmental reputation. The facts tell a very different story — one of renewal, recycling, and responsible stewardship.

Myth

"Printing destroys forests."

Fact

The paper industry depends on forest regeneration. US forest area has remained stable or increased. Paper companies plant more trees than they harvest. FSC, SFI, and PEFC certifications ensure responsible sourcing. Source: Two Sides NA ↗

Myth

"Going digital is always greener."

Fact

Data centers, devices, and streaming consume enormous energy 24/7. A printed brochure requires zero electricity to read, can last decades, and is fully recyclable. Digital has a significant — and often invisible — carbon footprint. Source: AF&PA ↗

Myth

"Paper ends up in landfills."

Fact

Paper is recycled at a higher rate than any other municipal solid waste material in the US — outperforming glass, metal, and plastic. 79% of Americans have curbside paper recycling. When paper does reach a landfill, it is biodegradable. Source: US EPA ↗

Soy-Based & Low-VOC Inks

Soy-based inks use a renewable feedstock and produce lower volatile organic compound (VOC) emissions than traditional petroleum-based inks. They also make paper easier to de-ink during recycling.
