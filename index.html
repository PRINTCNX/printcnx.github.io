Our Story

Built on Quality.
Driven by Relationships.

In commercial printing since 1983 — rooted in Los Angeles for decades, based in Newbury Park since 2019. The Printing Connection opens the door for you to develop and launch an entire campaign from start to finish. Marketing professionals are available for consultation to help you evaluate your marketing objectives and form a strategy with the collateral materials necessary to take your company to new levels of success.

From Conejo Valley businesses to clients across Southern California and the Pacific Northwest — every industry, every size — united by their demand for quality that represents their brand at the highest level.

We're not an online vending machine. Real people answer the phone, review your files, and work with you on custom needs — because your project deserves a team that cares about how it comes out, not just that it ships.

Quality Without Compromise Every job produced with obsessive attention to color, stock, and finish.
Partnership Over Transaction We learn your brand standards and deliver — every time.
