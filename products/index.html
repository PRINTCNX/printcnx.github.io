Prepress Preparation

Setting Up Your Files For Flawless Output

Our prepress department reviews every file before output. Following these technical standards ensures accurate trim, true color, and rapid turnaround.

300 DPI Resolution

Provide images and raster assets at 300 DPI minimum at final output dimensions to prevent pixelation or blur.

CMYK Color Space

Convert all RGB elements to CMYK prior to export. Specify Pantone PMS spot colors for strict corporate identity matching.

1/8″ Bleed All Sides

Extend all background images, colors, and art 0.125″ past the final trim edge so cutting leaves clean, borderless edges.

1/8″ Safe Zone

Keep all critical text, logos, barcodes, and borders at least 0.125″ (0.25″ recommended) inside the final cutting boundary.

Outlined Fonts & Vector

Convert all text and typography to outlines/curves in Illustrator and InDesign, or embed all fonts in your exported PDF.

Press-Ready PDF/X

Export as high-quality PDF/X-1a or PDF/X-4 with crop marks enabled. We always provide a digital proof before running.

What is the difference between digital printing and offset printing for my products?

Digital printing is ideal for short to medium runs (from 25 up to 1,000 pieces), variable data personalization, and same-day or 24-hour rush turnarounds. Offset printing uses aluminum printing plates and liquid inks, providing the lowest unit cost for higher quantities (1,000+ pieces) along with microscopic dot accuracy and exact Pantone PMS color matching.

How should I prepare my artwork files to ensure print-ready results?

We recommend submitting high-resolution print-ready PDFs at 300 DPI in the CMYK color space. Please include 1/8″ (0.125″) bleed on all edges and keep all important text, logos, and critical content at least 1/8″ inside the trim line (safe zone). Convert all fonts to outlines or curves prior to exporting.
