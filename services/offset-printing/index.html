Why Offset

Six Offset Advantages

When quality, volume, and brand precision matter — offset printing is the answer.

True Pantone PMS Colors

Offset uses real Pantone inks — not CMYK simulations. Critical for brand standards and regulated color accuracy.

Exceptional Print Quality

Offset lithography produces sharper dot gain, finer detail, and more consistent ink density than any digital press at comparable size.

Volume Economics

At 1,000 pieces and above, offset delivers dramatically lower cost-per-unit than digital. At 10,000+, the savings are substantial.

Premium Coatings

Soft-touch matte, high-gloss UV, spot UV, aqueous, and foil stamping — the full range of premium coatings is exclusive to offset runs.

Wide Substrate Range

Offset prints on virtually any paper from 40 lb uncoated to 100 lb cover, specialty stocks, plastics, and even metallic papers.

Soy & Vegetable Inks

We use low-VOC soy-based inks on offset runs — better for the environment, better for recyclability, and specified by many eco-conscious brands.

FAQs

Offset Printing Questions

When should I choose offset over digital?

Choose offset when: (1) Your quantity is over 1,000 pieces, (2) You need true Pantone PMS spot colors, (3) You want premium coatings like soft-touch, foil, or spot UV, or (4) You need the absolute finest print quality for high-end presentations or catalogs.

What is the typical turnaround for offset printing?

Standard offset is 7–10 business days after proof approval. This accounts for plate-making, press setup, press time, drying, and bindery/finishing. Rush service is available for select projects — call to discuss.

Do you provide press proofs?

Yes. For color-critical offset runs, we strongly recommend a press proof (also called an Epson proof or contract proof). This is a full-size, color-calibrated proof on the actual substrate that shows you exactly what will come off the press.

What is GraCol color calibration?

GraCol is a color calibration specification based on ICC color management standards. It defines how printing devices should reproduce color to deliver consistent, accurate results. We calibrate our presses weekly using the GraCol profile — no certification required, just rigorous process. Your brand colors are matched accurately on every run and every reorder.

Can you match colors from another printer?

Yes. Provide us with a Pantone guide number or a press proof/sample from your previous printer. We will do a color comparison and dial in our inks to match as closely as possible. True Pantone spot ink matching is available on offset runs.
