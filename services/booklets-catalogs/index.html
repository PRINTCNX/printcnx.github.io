Booklet & Catalog Printing Built for Volume

Saddle-stitch, perfect-bound, and case-bound booklets and catalogs — serving Ventura County, Greater Los Angeles, and nationwide programs with in-house bindery and full print management.

Booklets and catalogs are where binding decisions make or break a project. Saddle-stitch for programs and catalogs up to ~64 pages; perfect bind for annual reports and product catalogs that need a printable spine; case binding for premium keepsake pieces. We handle all of it in-house — no outsourcing to a third-party bindery.

For high-volume catalog programs, our offset production partners deliver consistent color across tens of thousands of copies, and our mailing services can take the piece straight from bindery to postal induction. Tell us your page count, quantity, and in-hands date and we'll spec the most cost-effective path.

We produce ongoing catalog and booklet programs for corporate, hospitality, and entertainment clients — including deadline-critical event programs.

FAQs

Booklet Printing Questions

What is your minimum order for booklets?

We work with runs as small as 25 copies for short-run digital, up to 100,000+ for high-volume offset. Our minimum order value is $100. We will recommend the right press for your quantity.

What file format do you accept?

We prefer print-ready PDF files with bleed and crop marks. We also accept InDesign (INDD), Illustrator (AI), and Photoshop (PSD) packages. Use our File Submission Guide for setup specs.

Do you offer design services?

We can connect you with our network of trusted local graphic designers. Alternatively, send us your InDesign files and we will do a preflight check at no charge.

How long does production take?

Standard turnaround is 5–7 business days after proof approval. Rush options (2–3 days) are available for digital runs under 1,000 copies. Call us to confirm availability.

How do you keep my colors consistent?

We calibrate our equipment weekly using the latest GRACoL® color profiles and run color consistency checks on every job — digital or offset. For brand-critical work we produce contract proofs for your approval, and we keep your color builds on file so reorders match. If a color is off, we reprint.

Do you ship nationwide?

Absolutely. We ship to clients across Southern California, the Pacific Northwest, and nationwide. We work with UPS, FedEx, and freight for large pallet deliveries.
