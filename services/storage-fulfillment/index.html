How It Works

Simple as Print → Store → Ship

01

Print Your Project With Us

Place your print order as normal. Tell your account representative you want to enroll in the storage program — they'll set up your inventory account at no charge.

02

We Receive & Catalog Your Inventory

When your job finishes, instead of shipping everything to you, we palletize, shrink-wrap, and receive it into our warehouse. Each item gets a SKU, a physical location, and an opening quantity in your inventory record.

03

We Monitor & Alert

Our team watches your inventory levels and sends you a proactive notification when any item drops below your agreed reorder point. Monthly inventory reports arrive automatically every month.

04

You Release On Demand

When you need materials, contact us by phone, email, or web portal with the item, quantity, and ship-to address. We pick, pack, and ship within one business day. No minimums.

05

Reorder Before You Run Out

When inventory reaches the reorder threshold, we coordinate a reprint, receive the new inventory, and seamlessly merge it into your existing storage record. Continuous availability, zero gaps.

FAQs

Storage Program Questions

Is storage really free with no hidden fees?

Yes — for any item we print and produce for you, storage in our facility is genuinely free. We offer this as a value-added service because long-term relationships benefit both parties. There are no monthly fees, no per-pallet charges, and no surprise billing. The only cost you pay is shipping when items are released.

How do I request a release from inventory?

Three ways: phone, email, or via your dedicated web portal (available for accounts with ongoing programs). Simply specify the item, quantity, and ship-to address. Releases received before 10am Pacific time typically ship same business day; after 10am ship next business day.

What is the minimum quantity I can release at one time?

There is no minimum. You can release a single envelope or a full pallet — we accommodate whatever your operational needs are. We understand that rush situations arise and single-piece releases to a branch office or customer are sometimes necessary.

Can you ship releases to multiple locations from one inventory?

Absolutely. Many clients use our storage program to centralize print purchasing and then distribute nationwide to regional offices, retail locations, distributors, or sales reps. We can ship simultaneous releases to hundreds of addresses from a single inventory record.

What if I need to update or reprint an item that is in storage?

We will handle a "version change" print order for the updated item and begin releasing from the new version. We will work with you on timing to deplete existing inventory where economical, or we can quarantine the old version if the change is urgent (compliance, legal, or brand change). Your account representative will guide you through the transition.

Can I store items that were not printed by The Printing Connection?

In general, our storage program is a benefit for clients who print with us. However, for high-volume accounts, we are open to discussing co-storage of related materials not produced in our facility. Contact your account representative to discuss your specific situation.
