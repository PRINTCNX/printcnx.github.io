What We Offer

Finishing Capabilities

Nine in-house finishing processes — all coordinated with your press run to eliminate hand-offs, reduce turnaround, and maintain quality control from print to delivery.

Saddle Stitching

The industry standard for booklets, catalogs, and magazines from 8 to 64 pages. Our Heidelberg stitcher/trimmer produces square, clean booklets with precise registration in one automated pass.

  • Booklets & catalogs
  • Event programs
  • Newsletters
  • Instruction manuals (thin)

Perfect Binding

Hot-glue spine binding for trade paperbacks, product catalogs, directories, and annual reports. Professional flat spine allows full cover-to-cover artwork. Ideal for 40+ page counts.

  • Product catalogs
  • Annual reports
  • Directories
  • Corporate presentations

Die Cutting & Embossing

Our Heidelberg Die Cutter SBB (22"×32") and Windmill handle precision die-cutting for custom shapes, pocket folders, and packaging. Embossing and debossing create dimensional tactile effects without ink.

  • Pocket & presentation folders
  • Custom-shaped pieces
  • Packaging & boxes
  • Premium business cards

Folding — All Styles

Two MBO folding machines handle any fold configuration: tri-fold, Z-fold (accordion), gate fold, roll fold, half-fold (bi-fold), French fold, and custom multi-panel configurations.

  • Tri-fold brochures
  • Accordion & Z-fold
  • Gate-fold mailers
  • Map & guide folding

3D Spot UV Coating (MGI JETvarnish)

Our MGI JETvarnish 3D UV Coater applies raised, dimensional spot UV varnish digitally — no dies needed. Creates tactile high-gloss highlights over matte substrates. Also produces foil-effect and glitter UV finishes.

  • Business card spot UV
  • Premium cover stock
  • Product packaging
  • Presentation folders

Aqueous (AQ) Coating

Inline aqueous coating applied on press on our offset runs. Protects printed surfaces Protects printed surfaces from scuffing and fingerprints. Available in matte, gloss, and satin finishes.

  • Book covers
  • Catalog covers
  • Postcards & mailers
  • Marketing collateral

Coil & Wire-O Binding

Plastic coil (spiral) and twin-loop Wire-O binding for presentations, planners, manuals, and look books. Lay-flat capability means pages open fully flat — essential for cookbooks, reference guides, and instruction manuals.

  • Presentations & look books
  • Planners & calendars
  • Technical manuals
  • Cookbooks & guides

Cutting & Trimming

Two programmable guillotine cutters provide accurate, repeatable trimming with CNC-level precision (±0.1mm). Used for business cards, post cards, booklets after binding, and all custom-cut formats.

  • Business cards

  • Custom paper sizes

Shrink Wrapping & Packaging

In-house shrink wrapping for bundled delivery, retail-ready packaging, and protected print storage. Handles single items, stacked bundle sets, and palletized runs for distribution.

Coatings & Laminates

Choose Your Surface Finish

The right coating can elevate a good print to an extraordinary one. Here are the six coating options we offer — and when to use each.

Aqueous Gloss

High-shine protective coating applied in-line on the offset press. Enhances color vibrancy and provides scratch resistance. Most common coating choice.

Aqueous Matte

Flat, non-reflective coating that gives a premium upscale feel. Reduces fingerprinting. Often combined with spot UV for dramatic contrast effect.

Aqueous Satin

Mid-sheen finish between gloss and matte. Provides color protection without excessive shine. Preferred for photo-heavy editorial pieces.

3D Spot UV (MGI JETvarnish)

Raised, dimensional UV varnish applied digitally to selected areas. Creates a tactile high-gloss effect over matte substrate. No dies required.

Soft-Touch Lamination

Velvety, tactile laminate film that feels premium to the touch. The "luxury" finish for high-end business cards, packaging, and brochure covers.

Gloss Lamination

Plastic film laminate that adds rigidity, durability, and high-gloss sheen. Excellent moisture resistance for pieces that will be handled repeatedly.

FAQs

Bindery & Finishing Questions

What is the difference between aqueous coating and lamination?

Aqueous (AQ) coating is a water-based liquid coating applied in-line on the press — it dries quickly and provides good scratch and scuff resistance at lower cost. Lamination is a plastic film bonded to the paper after printing — it is more durable, waterproof, and available in soft-touch and gloss finishes. Lamination also adds rigidity, making it ideal for business cards and book covers that see heavy use.

What is Spot UV and why is it so popular?

Spot UV (ultraviolet) coating is a high-gloss varnish applied only to selected areas of a printed piece — typically a logo, headline, or image — while the surrounding area remains matte. The contrast between the glossy and matte surface creates a dramatic, premium look that catches light and draws the eye. Our MGI JETvarnish system can also produce raised 3D spot UV, where the varnish builds up to create a tactile, dimensional texture.

Can you die-cut a custom shape?

Yes. Our Heidelberg Die Cutter SBB (22"×32") handles custom die-cutting for business cards, pocket folders, hang tags, packaging boxes, and any custom shape you design. We can create new custom dies or work with your existing die. Ask your sales representative for a quote — die cost is a one-time setup fee, and the die can be reused on future orders.

What is the minimum page count for saddle stitch vs. perfect bind?

Saddle stitching works best for 8 to 64 pages (must be a multiple of 4). Perfect binding requires a minimum of approximately 40 pages (about 3/16" spine width) to hold the glue properly. For 65+ page counts, perfect binding is generally the better option as it produces a flat, professional spine.

Do you offer in-house mailing and addressing for folded direct mail?

Yes. We handle the complete direct mail workflow in-house: printing, folding, inserting (if applicable), inkjet addressing, USPS indicia application, and drop-ship to your local post office. EDDM (Every Door Direct Mail) and targeted mailing list campaigns both available. See our Direct Mail service page for details.
