The right substrate makes the difference between a print that lasts and one that fades. We'll guide you to the correct material for your application, budget, and lifespan.

File Setup

Setting Up Your Wide Format Files

Resolution

Use 150–200 DPI at final print size for banners and vinyl. 300 DPI for foam board and smaller prints.

Color Mode

Set your document to CMYK. RGB files will be converted, which can shift colors. Pantone PMS available on request.

Bleed

Include a minimum 1/8" bleed on all sides. For banners with hems or grommets, our team will add the appropriate finishing area.

File Format

PDF (preferred), high-res AI or EPS with fonts outlined, or PSD at correct resolution. No Word or PowerPoint files please.

Safety Zone

Keep all critical text and logos 1/4" from the trim edge. Wide-format hardware can have slight shift at the edge.

Proof

We send a digital soft proof before printing. For color-critical work, request a hard proof — additional fee may apply.

FAQs

Wide Format Questions

What is the maximum print width?

We can print up to 10 feet wide on vinyl and fabric substrates. For larger applications (building wraps, full-wall murals), we tile and seam panels seamlessly.

What resolution is required for wide format files?

We recommend 150–200 DPI at final output size for vinyl banners, and 300 DPI for smaller items like foam board or window film. Our prepress team checks every file and will advise if your resolution is insufficient.

Do you offer installation services?

We partner with certified installers for vehicle wraps and large-scale wall/window installations in the Greater Los Angeles and Ventura County area. Ask us for a referral when requesting your quote.

Can I reuse my retractable banner hardware?

Yes. If you already have retractable banner stands, we can print replacement graphics to fit most major hardware brands (Expand, Tradeshow Display Warehouse, etc.). Send us the hardware specs.

How long do outdoor vinyl banners last?

Our UV-ink banners on 13 oz vinyl typically last 1–2 years outdoors. 18 oz with UV laminate can last 2–4 years depending on UV exposure and weather. We can add a UV overlaminate for extended life.

What file types do you accept?

PDF (preferred), AI, EPS with fonts outlined. For vehicle wraps, we supply dieline templates. Please include 1/8" bleed on all sides.
