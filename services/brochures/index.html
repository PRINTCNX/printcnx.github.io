Brochure Printing That Opens Conversations

Full-color brochure printing serving Ventura County, Greater Los Angeles, and clients nationwide — offset and digital with all fold styles and premium finishing options.

A brochure is often the first physical thing a prospect holds from your brand — so the fold, the stock, and the color all carry weight. We produce tri-fold, bi-fold, gate-fold, Z-fold, and custom brochures for marketing teams, hospitality groups, and corporate departments that need collateral to look as considered as the brand behind it.

Short runs and personalized versions go on our Konica Minolta digital presses; volume programs run offset through our production partners for the lowest per-piece cost and exact Pantone color. We'll recommend the right process based on your quantity, timeline, and how the piece will be used — not what's easiest for us.

Fold Styles

Six Brochure Formats

From the classic tri-fold to the dramatic gate fold — choose the format that best presents your message.

2 panels

Half-Fold (Bi-Fold)

A single center fold creating 2 panels (4 pages). The simplest and most popular format — ideal for corporate capability statements, newsletters, and rate sheets.

3 panels

Tri-Fold (Letter Fold)

The classic 8.5×11 letter fold — two parallel folds bringing all three panels inward. Fits standard pockets and racks. Ideal for service menus, hotel amenity guides, and sales sheets.

3 panels

Z-Fold

Three panels with alternating fold directions creating a Z shape. Unlike tri-fold, each panel opens independently — great for sequential stories, maps, and step-by-step guides.

5 panels

Accordion Fold

Four or more panels that unfold in alternating directions like a concertina. Can extend to any number of panels. Perfect for timelines, panoramic images, and multi-step instructions.

4 panels

Gate Fold

Two outer panels fold inward to meet at the center like double doors, revealing a dramatic full-width spread. Premium choice for product launches, invitations, and luxury mailers.

4 panels

Roll Fold

Three or more folds all going in the same direction — each panel rolls over the next like a scroll. Used for real estate brochures, travel guides, and instruction booklets.

4 panels

French Fold (Right-Angle)

The sheet is folded vertically, then folded again horizontally at a right angle — creating 4 quadrant panels and an elegant enclosed pocket. Popular for premium invitations, greeting cards, and maps.

4 panels

Double Parallel Fold

The sheet is folded in half, then folded in half again in the same direction — both folds parallel. Creates a compact 8-page piece. Common for pharmaceutical inserts, instruction leaflets, and travel brochures.

4 panels

Reverse Double Parallel

Similar to the double parallel fold, but the second fold reverses direction — creating a different panel reading order when opened. Used for multi-section content where the reveal sequence matters.

4 panels

Double Gatefold

A standard gate fold where both outer gate panels open inward, then the entire piece folds in half again. Creates a dramatic multi-stage reveal with 8 pages. Ideal for luxury brochures and premium event programs.

Custom Sizes

DL (mail size), 4×9, 5×7, and any custom dimension — any fold style, any page count. Our team will work with you on any format that best serves your message.

FAQs

Brochure Printing Questions

What is the most popular brochure style?

The tri-fold (letter-fold) on 100 lb gloss text with aqueous coating is our most ordered brochure. It is versatile, fits standard racks and pockets, and reproduces photos beautifully.

Can you match our corporate colors?

Yes. We offer Pantone PMS spot color matching on offset runs, and our digital presses are calibrated weekly using the latest GRACoL® color profiles for tight CMYK matches. Request a press proof for color-critical brand work.

Do you offer mailing services?

We can prepare your brochures for EDDM (Every Door Direct Mail) or targeted list mailing. We partner with local mail houses for list procurement and postage discounts.

Can I order samples before my full run?

Yes. Request a 10-piece proof run (additional cost) before committing to large quantities. We highly recommend this for new designs or specialty coatings.

What is your minimum order?

Our minimum order value is $100. For brochures, this typically means 100–250 copies on the digital press. Higher quantities on offset presses offer significantly better per-unit pricing.
