What We Offer

10 In-House Design Capabilities

Everything from concept through print-ready file — all under one roof. No hand-off to an outside agency, no miscommunication between designer and printer.

Concept Development

We work with you from the initial idea through creative brief development, mood boards, and design direction. Our in-house creatives help translate your marketing goals into a visual concept before a single pixel is designed.

Graphic Design

Full-service graphic design for print and digital: brochures, ads, catalogs, posters, signage, and more. We design in the correct format for print output — CMYK, correct bleed, print-ready files delivered every time.

Logo Development & Brand Identity

New business? Rebrand? We develop logos and complete brand identity systems — primary logo, color palette, typography, usage guidelines, and all file formats (AI, EPS, PNG, SVG). Your brand, built to last.

Digital Restoration

Restore old, damaged, or low-resolution photos and artwork to print-quality resolution. We work with scanned photos, old logos, negatives, and deteriorated files — recovering usable print-ready art from nearly any source.

File Reconstruction & Repair

Don't have the original design files? We can reconstruct logos, brochures, and marketing materials from a print sample or PDF. Converted to editable, print-native format (AI, INDD, or EPS) for future use.

Wide Format Proof Design

Design services specifically for wide-format output: banners, trade show displays, vehicle wraps, and environmental graphics. We account for viewing distance, resolution, and substrate in every wide-format design we create.

Print & Web Optimization

Files prepared for both print and digital distribution in a single workflow. We deliver print-ready PDFs plus web-optimized PNG/JPEG/SVG versions simultaneously, ensuring consistent brand presentation across all channels.

Product Photography

In-house product photography for catalogs, e-commerce, and marketing collateral. Controlled studio environment with consistent lighting — from single-item shots to full catalog runs. Images delivered print-ready and web-sized.

Duplication — CD, DVD & Flash

Short-run CD, DVD, and USB flash drive duplication with full-color printing on disc and in-house designed packaging. Ideal for software, training materials, portfolios, and conference kits.

Medium Format Scanning

High-resolution flatbed and drum scanning for medium-format negatives, transparencies, and photographic prints. Output at 300–600 DPI at final print size. Archive quality preservation for historical photos and art.

Our Process

From Brief to Press-Ready in 5 Steps

01

Discovery & Brief

We start with a conversation about your business, your audience, and the goals for the piece. No design begins until we understand what success looks like for you.

02

Concept Presentation

We present two or three distinct creative directions. You choose a direction, provide feedback, and we refine. No guesswork — you approve before we finalize anything.

03

Design Development

Full design development with copy integration, imagery selection or photography, and color system application. You review each round with clear, annotated revision notes.

04

Proofing & Approval

Digital soft proof (and hard proof for color-critical work). You sign off before we commit to plate or file output. Catch anything — that's what proofs are for.

05

Print-Ready File Delivery

Final files delivered as print-ready PDFs plus source files in your requested format. You own the files — they're yours to use with any vendor in the future.

FAQs

Design Service Questions

Do I need to bring you a print-ready file, or can you design from scratch?

Either way works. We accept finished, print-ready files in PDF, AI, INDD, PSD, and most industry-standard formats. We also take on design projects from a blank canvas — from logo development to complete brochure campaigns. Most clients fall somewhere in between: they have an idea or rough layout, and our team helps refine and prepare it for production.

What file formats do you accept for print-ready artwork?

We accept PDF/X-1a and PDF/X-4 (preferred), Adobe Illustrator (AI), InDesign (INDD with package), Photoshop (PSD), EPS with fonts outlined, and high-resolution TIFF. We can also work with Word and PowerPoint files — though we strongly recommend native print files for best quality. Our prepress team reviews every file before it goes to plate.

Can you match our Pantone PMS brand colors?

Yes. Pantone PMS color matching is available on all offset press runs. Our 5-color and 6-color Heidelberg presses support spot color printing for precise PMS matching that CMYK process printing cannot achieve. For digital press runs, we use Color-managed CMYK simulation of PMS colors — accurate for most brand applications. Ask your rep for a color proof if PMS accuracy is critical.

Do I own the files after design is complete?

Yes. For design work we produce for you, the final files are yours upon payment. We deliver print-ready PDFs and, upon request, the native source files (AI, INDD, PSD). You are free to take those files to any vendor. We keep a copy on file for reorders — simply call us and we can reprint from our archived files without any file setup fee.

How long does a typical design project take?

Timeline varies by project complexity. A single-sided business card design takes 1–2 business days. A multi-page product catalog design typically runs 2–4 weeks depending on content scope and revision rounds. Rush design services are available at an additional fee. Your account representative will give you a specific timeline estimate during the project brief.
