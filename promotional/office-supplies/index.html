Buyer Guide & FAQs

Branded Office Supplies FAQs

What are the minimum orders for branded pens, notebooks, and desk items?

Executive padfolios start at 12–24 pieces, debossed journals and mouse pads start at 25 pieces, and promotional pens and sticky notes typically start at 100–250 pieces. Custom presentation pocket folders are produced starting at 50–100 pieces.

How are logos applied to notebooks, padfolios, and metal pens?

We recommend blind debossing or metallic foil stamping for leatherette journals and padfolios, permanent laser engraving for anodized metal pens and tech hubs, and full-color dye sublimation for edge-to-edge mouse pads and desk mats.

Can you assemble and ship employee onboarding or client welcome kits?

Yes! Through our Newbury Park kitting and fulfillment team, we bundle branded pens, debossed notebooks, custom pocket folders, and welcome letters into custom-printed mailer boxes and ship them directly to new hires or clients.

How long does production take for branded office supplies?

Standard production is 7–12 business days after digital proof approval, with 3–5 day rush programs available on select pens, mouse pads, and notepads. Call (800) 786-5490 for rush availability.
