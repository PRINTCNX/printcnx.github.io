Product Range

Bags & Totes for Every Purpose

From lightweight trade show giveaways to premium executive gifts — we source and brand the right bag for your campaign and budget.

Canvas Tote Bags

Heavy-duty natural canvas totes with screen-printed or embroidered logos. The go-to sustainable giveaway.

  • 10 oz natural canvas
  • Screen print or embroidery
  • Standard & long handle
  • Minimum 25 pcs

Non-Woven Totes

Lightweight polypropylene totes ideal for trade shows and retail giveaways. Full-color printing on both sides.

  • 80–120 gsm polypropylene
  • Full-color print
  • Reinforced handles
  • Low minimum orders

Backpacks

Branded backpacks for corporate gifts, school promotions, and trade show prize packages.

  • Polyester & nylon options
  • Screen print or embroidery
  • Multiple pockets
  • One-size standard

Drawstring Bags

Budget-friendly drawstring cinch bags perfect for gym giveaways, school events, and youth programs.

  • Polyester 210D
  • Full-color print
  • Cinch closure
  • Minimum 24 pcs

Cooler Bags & Totes

Insulated cooler bags and lunch totes for outdoor events, picnics, and high-value client gifts.

  • PEVA insulated lining
  • Screen print or emb.
  • Zipper & open-top styles
  • Great gift item

Grocery & Produce Bags

Eco-friendly reusable shopping bags that keep your brand in rotation at grocery stores and markets.

  • Recycled RPET options
  • Folds into pouch
  • Machine washable
  • Low cost per piece

Briefcase & Portfolio Bags

Executive-grade padfolio bags and briefcases for premium corporate gifts and conference attendees.

  • Faux leather & canvas
  • Embossed or printed logo
  • Tablet & document pockets
  • Gift box ready

Specialty & Die-Cut Bags

Shaped, die-cut, or laminated bags for retail packaging and branded unboxing experiences.

  • Custom shapes available
  • Laminated finishes
  • Rigid & soft options
  • Full custom spec
