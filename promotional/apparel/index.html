Decoration Methods

The Right Technique for Every Job

We'll help you choose the decoration method that best suits your garment, logo style, quantity, and budget.

Screen Printing

Best for large runs with 1–8 spot colors. Extremely durable ink bonds directly to the fabric for long-lasting results.

Best for: T-shirts, hoodies, events

Embroidery

Raised thread work that looks and feels premium. The gold standard for polos, hats, and corporate uniforms.

Best for: Polos, hats, jackets

Dye Sublimation

Full-color all-over printing that becomes part of the fabric. Photographic detail, vibrant gradients, no cracking.

Best for: Performance wear, all-over prints

Heat Transfer Vinyl

Ideal for names, numbers, and small runs. Precise cuts, solid colors, and fast turnaround for custom individual pieces.

Best for: Small runs, personalization
