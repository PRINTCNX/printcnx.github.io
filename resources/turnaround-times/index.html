Policy Details

Understanding Your Turnaround

Five things every client should know about how our turnaround policy works so there are no surprises on deadline day.

Clock Starts at Proof Approval

Turnaround time does not begin when files are received; it begins the moment you approve your proof. If a job is placed on hold, has revised files, or is awaiting approval, the clock pauses.

5:00 PM Shipping Cutoff

Orders that complete production after 5:00 PM Pacific may not be handed to the carrier until the following business day. If same-day ship is critical, communicate this when placing the order.

Bindery Time Is Additive

Press turnaround times are based on flat sheet printing only. If your job requires folding, saddle stitching, perfect binding, die-cutting, or other finishing, add 2 business days to the press time.

PDF Proofs Included on All Jobs

A digital PDF proof is provided at no charge for every job before it goes to press. Hard proofs (physical press proofs) are available as an added service. Proofing time is not in the turnaround window.

Rush Is Schedule-Dependent

Rush service is available on an as-needed basis. We do not guarantee rush availability, so always call ahead to confirm a rush slot before counting on an accelerated timeline.

FAQs

Turnaround Time Questions

When does my turnaround clock actually start?

Your turnaround time starts the moment you give written approval on your proof, not when you submit files. Revision rounds, holds, and waiting on your approval all pause the clock.

Does the turnaround time include bindery finishing?

No. Our published turnaround times cover flat-sheet printing only. If your job requires bindery work (folding, saddle stitching, perfect binding, die-cutting, coil binding, or any other finishing), add 2 business days to the press completion time.

What is the difference between digital and offset turnaround?

Our Newbury Park digital presses require no plate-making, so approved jobs can be running within hours. Commercial sheetfed offset lithography requires plate production, ink mixing, press makeready, and color control pulls under our prepress and press-check oversight, which accounts for the 3–5 business day window.

Can I get a same-day or overnight turn?

Yes, on digital jobs with approved files received before 10:00 AM Pacific. Rush service is subject to current production capacity. Call us at (800) 786-5490 before placing a rush order. Rush fees apply.

What if I miss the 5:00 PM cutoff?

If your job completes production after 5:00 PM, it may not be tendered to the shipping carrier until the next business day. Communicate your hard deadline when you place the order.

Does shipping time count as part of turnaround?

No. Our turnaround time ends when your job ships from our facility. For local Newbury Park and Los Angeles area clients, same-day or next-day local delivery is often available.

Can you split a job to ship part now and the rest later?

Yes. For large jobs where you need a portion for an event, we can arrange a partial shipment. Coordinate this with your account representative when placing the order.
