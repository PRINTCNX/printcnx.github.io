Policy Details

Understanding Your Turnaround

Five things every client should know about how our turnaround policy works — so there are no surprises on deadline day.

Clock Starts at Proof Approval

Turnaround time does not begin when files are received — it begins the moment you approve your proof. If a job is placed on hold, has revised files, or is awaiting your approval signature, the turnaround clock pauses until approval is given.

5:00 PM Shipping Cutoff

Orders that complete production after 5:00 PM Pacific may not be handed to the carrier until the following business day. If a same-day ship is critical, communicate this to your account representative when placing your order.

Bindery Time Is Additive

Press turnaround times are based on flat sheet printing only and do not include bindery finishing time. If your job requires folding, saddle stitching, perfect binding, die-cutting, or other finishing, add 2 business days (standard) to the press completion time.

PDF Proofs Included on All Jobs

A digital PDF proof is provided at no charge for every job before it goes to press. Hard proofs (physical press proofs) are available as an added service — ask your representative for pricing. Proofing time is not included in the turnaround window.

Rush Is Schedule-Dependent

Rush service is available on an as-needed basis, and turnaround is based on current production capacity. We do not guarantee rush availability — always call ahead to confirm a rush slot before counting on an accelerated timeline.

FAQs

Turnaround Time Questions

When does my turnaround clock actually start?

Your turnaround time starts the moment you give written approval on your proof — not when you submit files. If you submit files and we send a proof, the clock starts when you reply approving that proof. Revision rounds, holds, and waiting on your approval all pause the clock. This protects both parties — it ensures your job gets full production time after a clean approval, not a compressed window created by revision cycles.

Does the turnaround time include bindery finishing?

No. Our published turnaround times cover flat-sheet printing only. If your job requires bindery work — folding, saddle stitching, perfect binding, die-cutting, coil binding, or any other finishing — add 2 business days to the press completion time. For jobs combining rush printing AND rush finishing, call ahead so we can coordinate both queues simultaneously.

What is the difference between digital and offset turnaround?

Digital printing (our Konica Minolta AccurioPress) requires no plate-making, so it can be running within hours of file approval. The 1-day turnaround on small digital runs is realistic for most jobs. Offset printing requires plate production, ink mixing, press makeready, and color control pulls — hence the longer 3–5 day window. For large-volume jobs where offset is cost-effective, this window is standard across the industry.

Can I get a same-day or overnight turn?

Yes, on digital jobs with approved files received before 10:00 AM Pacific. Rush service is subject to current production capacity — we cannot guarantee a rush slot without confirmation. Call us at 800-786-5490 before placing a rush order. Rush fees apply and vary based on job size and current schedule.

What if I miss the 5:00 PM cutoff?

If your job completes production after 5:00 PM, it may not be tendered to the shipping carrier until the next business day. If you have a hard in-hands deadline, build this into your timeline and communicate your deadline when you place the order. We'll let you know if the timeline is achievable before production starts.

Does shipping time count as part of turnaround?

No. Our turnaround time ends when your job ships from our facility. Transit time to your location depends on your carrier and service level selected. For local Newbury Park and Los Angeles area clients, same-day or next-day local delivery is often available — ask your representative.

Can you split a job — ship part now and the rest later?

Yes. For large jobs where you need a portion for an event and the remainder later, we can arrange a partial shipment from storage. Coordinate this with your account representative when placing the order so we can plan the run accordingly.
