Choosing the Right Fold

The fold you choose determines how your reader experiences your content — the order panels are revealed, how compact the finished piece is, how it mails, and how premium it feels. Each fold style has distinct strengths and design constraints.

This guide covers every fold we produce at The Printing Connection, with technical diagrams showing exactly how each sheet folds, panel counts, and best-use recommendations from decades of production experience. If you are unsure which format is right for your project, we are happy to provide a physical folding dummy sample before you finalize your design.

Half-Fold (Bi-Fold)

Most Common
1 fold · 2 panels · 4 pages

A single center fold creates two equal panels and four printable pages. The most widely used and economical brochure format — straightforward to design, quick to fold, and easy to mail.

Best for:
  • Corporate capability statements
  • Rate sheets and price lists
  • Event programs
  • Newsletters
  • Letters with inserts
Ideal for designs with a clear cover and inside spread. Images can bleed across the full inside spread for dramatic impact.

Tri-Fold (Letter Fold)

Most Popular
2 folds · 3 panels · 6 pages

Two parallel folds bring the left and right thirds inward over the center panel in a letter-folding pattern. Finished piece is 1/3 the original sheet width — fits standard pockets, racks, and #10 envelopes.

Best for:
  • Restaurant takeout menus
  • Hotel amenity guides
  • Retail sales sheets
  • Travel brochures
  • Real estate handouts
The inner right panel is seen first when opened — use it for a strong secondary message or call-to-action.

Z-Fold

Sequential
2 folds · 3 panels · 6 pages

Two folds in alternating directions create a zigzag Z shape when viewed from the side. Each panel is independent — the piece can be fully extended or viewed panel by panel. Great for storytelling with a clear sequence.

Best for:
  • Maps and wayfinding
  • Step-by-step instructions
  • Before/During/After stories
  • Triptych photography
  • Sequential product reveals
The alternating fold lets the piece stand on its own like a tent card when positioned on edge — useful for table displays.

Accordion Fold

Multi-Panel
4 folds · 5 panels · 10 pages

Four or more folds alternate direction like an accordion or concertina. Any number of equal panels can be added. The fully extended piece shows all panels simultaneously as one long panoramic format.

Best for:
  • Long timelines and roadmaps
  • Panoramic photography
  • Multi-chapter product stories
  • Museum and gallery guides
  • Extended instructional content
Accordion folds can be oriented vertically or horizontally. Ensure critical content is visible both folded and fully extended.

Gate Fold

Premium
2 folds · 4 panels · 8 pages

Two outer panels fold inward to meet at the center — like double doors opening to reveal a dramatic full-width spread. The center spread is typically twice the width of each gate panel, creating an impressive panoramic reveal.

Best for:
  • Product launches
  • Luxury invitations and announcements
  • Annual report covers
  • Premium direct mail
  • High-end restaurant menus
Gate panels must be slightly narrower than half the center spread to avoid buckling — typically 1/16"–1/8" per side.

Roll Fold

Compact
3 folds · 4 panels · 8 pages

Three or more folds all go in the same direction — each successive panel rolls over the previous like a scroll. The innermost panel must be slightly narrower to account for paper thickness and prevent buckling.

Best for:
  • Real estate property brochures
  • Multi-destination travel guides
  • Product comparison brochures
  • Instruction booklets
  • Pharmaceutical inserts
Panel widths should decrease slightly from outermost to innermost (approx. 1/16" each). Our prepress team handles this automatically.

French Fold (Right-Angle)

Premium
2 folds · 4 panels · 8 pages

The sheet is folded in half vertically, then again horizontally at a right angle — creating four equal quadrant panels. The open edges meet at one corner, creating an elegant enclosed pocket.

Best for:
  • Premium event invitations
  • Greeting cards
  • Announcement cards
  • Maps and diagrams
  • Luxury mailers
French fold is often printed one side only — the fold creates the "inside" naturally. This reduces cost while maintaining a premium appearance.

Double Parallel Fold

Informational
2 folds · 4 panels · 8 pages

The sheet is folded in half, then folded in half again in the same direction — both folds are parallel. Creates a compact 8-page piece 1/4 the original sheet width. Reading order unfolds sequentially from front to back.

Best for:
  • Pharmaceutical package inserts
  • Technical instruction leaflets
  • Software documentation
  • Multi-destination travel itineraries
  • Detailed event schedules
The parallel fold creates a clear front-to-back reading sequence — ideal when information must be consumed in a specific order.

Reverse Double Parallel

Informational
2 folds · 4 panels · 8 pages

Similar to double parallel fold, but the second fold reverses direction — changing which panel is encountered first and last when the piece is opened. Creates a different panel reveal order.

Best for:
  • Dual-language documents
  • Before/After content with shared center
  • Multi-product comparison pieces
  • Proposals with summary on outer panels
  • Mirror-structure content
Ask us for a folding dummy before finalizing your design — the reversed fold significantly changes the panel reading order.

Double Gatefold

Luxury
3 folds · 4 panels · 8 pages

A standard gate fold enhanced with an additional center fold — the gated piece folds in half again. Creates a dramatic two-stage opening experience: first the piece unfolds, then the gates open to reveal the full center spread.

Best for:
  • Annual reports and investor pieces
  • Luxury product launches
  • High-end wedding invitations
  • Exclusive event programs
  • Prestige brand campaigns
Inner gate panels must be slightly narrower than outer panels to prevent buckling. Allow at least 1/8" clearance. Production cost is higher — best for premium projects where the reveal experience matters.

Custom Sizes & Formats

Ask Us

Beyond standard formats, we produce brochures in virtually any size and fold combination: DL (mail size), 4×9, 5×7, square formats, mini-brochures, and unique custom configurations. We can also combine fold styles or add die-cut elements.

Best for:
  • DL (99×210mm) mail-standard pieces
  • 4×9" standard rack display
  • 5×7" square and portrait formats
  • Die-cut shapes combined with folds
  • Large-format fold-out posters
Have an unusual format in mind? We'll create a physical folding dummy sample before you commit to a full print run — at no charge for qualified projects.
