Press Standards

Template Specifications & Standards

Every template in our library is built to these strict press standards. Follow these guidelines to ensure accurate trimming, clean folding, and seamless finished prints.

Magenta

Trim Line

Cut Edge

The exact finished cut line of your printed piece. All artwork intended to reach the sheet edge must extend completely past this line into the bleed.

Green

Score / Fold Line

Fold & Crease

Indicates where paper is scored and folded (bi-fold, tri-fold, gate fold, etc.). Keep vital text and critical logos away from fold creases to prevent cracking or misalignment.

1/8" (.125")

Standard Bleed

Press Standard

Artwork, background colors, and edge graphics must extend 1/8" (0.125″) past the trim line on all four sides to prevent unprinted white edges during precision cutting.

1/8" to 1/4"

Safe Area Margin

Recommended

Keep all essential content (text, logos, contact info) at least 1/8" (0.125″) from the edge of the sheet — 1/4" (0.25″) is strongly recommended for visual balance and cutting tolerance.

Template Specifications

PDF Templates

  • Acrobat-compatible layered PDF
  • Layers: Bleed, Trim, Safe Area, Die/Fold, Content (editable)
  • Color mode: CMYK
  • Resolution guide: 300 ppi minimum for photos
  • Open in Acrobat, Affinity Publisher, or Canva Pro

Illustrator (.ai) Templates

  • Compatible with Adobe CC 2020 and newer
  • Organized layers with locked guides
  • CMYK document color space
  • Swatches include Registration, None, and bleed guide colors
  • Artboard set to trim size; bleed configured in Document Setup

InDesign (.indd) Templates

  • Compatible with Adobe CC 2020 and newer
  • Document bleed: 0.125″ all sides
  • Slug area includes fold and die-line notes
  • Paragraph & Character styles included
  • Package the file before sending (File → Package)
