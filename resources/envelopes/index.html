Envelope Printing Tips & USPS Guidelines

Bleed & Safe Area

When printing envelope shells, keep all critical content at least 0.25″ from any edge. The flap area requires extra clearance — always use our die template files for exact printable zones.

Paper Weight

Most commercial envelopes are manufactured from 24 lb. Bond (60 lb. Text) stock. For premium pieces, 28 lb. Bond (70 lb. Text) or specialty converted-kraft stocks are common.

Print Methods

Envelopes can be printed offset, digitally, or via thermography. Window areas and seam placement restrict printable zones — always use our template files before submitting artwork.

USPS Compliance

Minimum mailable envelope: 3½″ × 5″, at least 0.007″ thick. Maximum letter-rate: 6⅛″ × 11½″. Square envelopes incur a non-machinable surcharge. Larger pieces mail at flat rates.

Address Placement

USPS requires the return address in the upper-left corner. For window envelopes, ensure the destination address shows clearly through the window — always test with a real insert before ordering.

Seal Types

Moisture-seal (gummed) flaps require wetting. Peel-and-seal (self-seal) strips are preferred for high-volume mailings. Latex-seal closures are compatible with automated inserting machines.
